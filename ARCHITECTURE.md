# SamudraAI ? Technical Architecture Document

## 1. Executive Summary
SamudraAI is an operational agentic decision-support system designed to fuse remote sensing data (ISRO Oceansat-3, INSAT-3D), oceanographic models (INCOIS OSF), meteorological advisories (IMD), and statutory maritime boundaries (ICG GIS) into actionable guidance for Indian coastal communities.

## 3. Specialized Agents

| Agent Name | Primary Responsibility | Input Artifacts | Output Artifacts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Planner Agent** | Parses query intent, identifies temporal target (current/tomorrow), detects language script, constructs subtask execution graph. | User query string, coordinate context | `ExecutionPlan` object |
| **Marine Data Discovery Agent** | Identifies appropriate datasets from Oceansat-3, MOSDAC, INCOIS, IMD; normalizes schemas. | Geographic sector coordinates | Normalized `MarineObservation` |
| **Weather Intelligence Agent** | Analyzes wind speed, gusts, swell period, convective lightning discharges, and IMD cyclone watches. | Coordinates, target timestamp | `WeatherReport` |
| **Ocean Analytics Agent** | Analyzes spaceborne Sea Surface Temperature (SST) gradients, Chlorophyll-a plumes, and Douglas sea scale state. | Coordinates | `MarineObservation` |
| **PFZ Intelligence Agent** | Detects thermal-chlorophyll frontal features and ranks zones by distance, environmental suitability, or transit safety. | Coordinates, sort preference | `List[PFZZone]` |
| **Geospatial Reasoning Agent** | Executes Haversine distance, bearing calculations, Ray-Casting point-in-polygon containment, and cross-track IMBL proximity. | Coordinates, Geofences | `BoundaryContext` |
| **Risk Assessment Agent** | Evaluates deterministic multi-factor physical safety penalties. Zero LLM score hallucination. | Weather, Ocean, Boundaries | `RiskAssessment` |
| **Route Optimization Agent** | Calculates direct rhumb line navigation versus risk-mitigated safe detour waypoints skirting hazard polygons. | Origin, Destination, Hazards | `RouteComparison` |
| **Marine Alert Agent** | Scans threshold breaches to issue prioritized alerts (Extreme, High, Moderate, Informational). | Weather, Boundaries, Waves | `List[MarineAlert]` |
| **Visualization Agent** | Determines which map layers, markers, and chart series must be activated in response to the user's intent. | Intent, Risk level | Map layer triggers |
| **Explanation & Evidence Agent** | Constructs the "Why am I seeing this?" audit trail and synthesizes multilingual natural language responses in 10 Indian tongues. | All agent artifacts | `ChatResponse` |

## 4. Deterministic Risk Engine

### 4.1. Core Mathematical Model
To guarantee human life safety in maritime operations, the overall risk score is calculated using a deterministic linear penalty matrix rather than generative AI probabilities:

$$	ext{Risk Score} = \sum_{i=1}^{6} w_i \cdot S_i$$

Where weights and factors are configured as:

| Factor ($i$) | Weight ($w_i$) | Safe Limit ($S_i \le 25$) | Moderate ($25 < S_i \le 50$) | High ($50 < S_i \le 75$) | Extreme ($S_i > 75$) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Wind Speed** | 0.20 | $< 25$ km/h | $25 - 40$ km/h | $40 - 55$ km/h | $> 55$ km/h |
| **Wave Height** | 0.25 | $< 1.8$ m | $1.8 - 2.5$ m | $2.5 - 4.0$ m | $> 4.0$ m |
| **Lightning Squalls** | 0.15 | None detected | Distal ($> 30$ km) | Approaching ($10-30$ km) | Active ($< 10$ km) |
| **Cyclone Watch** | 0.25 | None | Notice / Advisory | Watch (Depression) | Warning (Cyclone) |
| **Geofencing / IMBL** | 0.10 | Safe distance | Buffer ($15-20$ km) | Approach ($5-15$ km) | Critical ($< 5$ km) or Inside |
| **Tidal Surges** | 0.05 | $< 2.0$ m | $2.0 - 2.8$ m | $2.8 - 3.5$ m | $> 3.5$ m spring surge |

### 4.2. Risk Classification
- **0 ? 25: LOW (SAFE)** ? Favourable operational conditions. Normal artisanal and commercial fishing permitted.
- **26 ? 50: MODERATE (SAFE WITH CAUTION)** ? Nearshore conditions manageable. Small motorized crafts advised to exercise vigilance.
- **51 ? 75: HIGH (UNSAFE)** ? Rough sea states and elevated wind/swell make offshore voyages hazardous.
- **76 ? 100: EXTREME (HAZARDOUS)** ? Severe weather, cyclone warning, or sovereign boundary breach. Stay in or return to port immediately.
